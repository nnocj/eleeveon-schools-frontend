Baseline: 4 November 2026. Source: uploaded src(20261104-040244).zip, schema(10).prisma, lib(20261104-040512).zip, and prior map.md.
Goal and design rules
One shared Eleeveon Schools product with separate axes for (1) deployment runtime, (2) payment/ownership arrangement, (3) base feature entitlement, (4) independently purchased modules, (5) version/update rights, and (6) optional services. Do not duplicate school academics, reports, finance, identity or sync engines. Maintain separation between school-collected fees and Eleeveon's commercial billing.

Target configurations: Cloud (recurring), Independent Essentials (single administrative installation), Independent Multi-device (later), purchased outright or under an installment-to-perpetual agreement where eligible. Future school-hosted deployments and packaged services remain extensible, not first-release blockers.
What is confirmed in the supplied code
- Prisma already has SubscriptionPlan, AccountSubscription, SubscriptionPeriod, SubscriptionChangeOrder, AccountEntitlement, PrivateOffer, PrivateOfferAssignment, AccountPricingOverride, Invoice, AppPayment, PaymentProviderEvent, PerpetualLicense, LicenseActivation, LicenseDevice, LicenseVersionEntitlement, LicenseUpgradeOffer, LicensePurchaseAgreement, LicenseInstallment, LicenseEntitlementToken, EnterpriseDeployment, BackupSnapshot, RestoreOperation, ProductReleaseArtifact and other relevant infrastructure.
- Backend already implements controllers/services under src/billing/, src/licenses/, src/entitlements/, src/sync/, and src/payment-gateway/. Paystack and manual payment provider implementations exist. Presence does not imply audited end-to-end reliability.
- Frontend lib/ has subscription/, db/access/, db/modules/commercial-access.tables.ts, offline/, sync/, and db/db-backup.ts.
- Important discrepancy: frontend commercial-access types represent deployment as connected | offline while Prisma plan and entitlement defaults use eleeveon_cloud, and other policy variants appear server-side. Normalize semantics through an explicit mapping; don't silently rename stored values.
- Significant issue: src/licenses/licenses.controller.ts licence issuance/suspension/revocation/upgrade endpoints use JwtAuthGuard, but lack visible role restrictions; src/billing/private-offers.controller.ts and src/billing/pricing-overrides.controller.ts also only show JWT protection. src/billing/billing.controller.ts has RolesGuard at controller level but no visible @Roles restrictions for its sensitive operations, making the guard permissive by default. Audit service checks too before confirming exploitability.
- src/licenses/license-crypto.service.ts presently signs licence state with HMAC, which is unsuitable for independently verifying offline licences in a client that must not receive the signing secret. Prefer asymmetric signatures and public-key verification.
- src/sync/subscription-sync-policy.service.ts documents a pre-entitlement compatibility fallback permitting full sync unless strict enforcement is enabled. Migrate accounts then remove this permissive fallback.
- The three upload bundles do not contain the complete frontend application (app/components, app/context, app/hooks, app/providers.tsx), native configs (capacitor.config.ts, next.config.ts, package.json), or all test/build configurations. Their existence and integration cannot be verified from this upload. Request these exact files only when their stage starts.
